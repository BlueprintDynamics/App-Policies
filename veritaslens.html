VeritasLens AI Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Terms of Use

VeritasLens analyses the files and links you choose and does not keep them. There are no accounts, no ad networks and no tracking. Your scan history and settings are saved on your device.

What you send for analysis

When you scan an image, video, audio clip, text or a web link, that content is sent to the VeritasLens server and analysed by Google's Gemini AI service to estimate whether it is AI-generated or manipulated.

  • Files and text are used only to produce your result and are not stored by VeritasLens afterwards.
  • For a social media or web link, the VeritasLens server opens the public page to fetch the media.
  • Google processes submitted content under the Gemini API Terms and Google's Privacy Policy.

Please only upload content you have the right to share, and avoid uploading sensitive images of other people.

Shared reports

If you tap Share on a report, a copy of that report (the result and details about the file, not the file itself) is held temporarily on the VeritasLens server so others can open it. Anyone with the link can view it. Shared reports are not kept permanently and are removed as newer reports are shared or when the server restarts.

Data stored on your device

Your scan history, notes on reports, plan choice and settings (such as theme and language) are saved on your device only. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes them.

Camera

VeritasLens uses your camera only when you choose to capture a photo for analysis. It does not access your contacts or location.

Advertising and tracking

VeritasLens does not use ad networks, analytics services or advertising identifiers. Any "sponsored" messages in the app are VeritasLens's own promotions and do not track you.

Technical logs

Like most web services, the server's hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability.

Accuracy

Results are estimates and can be wrong. Do not rely on them alone for legal, financial or safety decisions.

Children

VeritasLens is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
