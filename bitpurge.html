Bitpurge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 15, 2026 · Terms of Use

Bitpurge does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

What Bitpurge reads on your phone

  • Photos, videos and audio (only with your permission), to find large files, duplicates, similar, blurry and dark photos, and old screenshots. To spot similar or blurry photos, Bitpurge makes a tiny fingerprint and a sharpness score from each photo's preview. These are calculated and kept on your phone only.
  • Folders you choose, if you add them on the Files & junk screen (for example Documents). Bitpurge reads the names, sizes and dates of files in those folders, and file contents only to confirm exact duplicates. You can remove a folder at any time. If you turn on auto-scan, Bitpurge rechecks these folders in the background every few days, on your phone only.
  • Files you pick in Android's file picker, to show their size and delete them if you ask.
  • App sizes and when you last used each app, only if you turn on "Usage access" for Bitpurge in Android Settings. This is used to show which apps take the most space.
  • Battery level, charging state, temperature and health reported by Android, to show on the Storage screen. These are read when the screen opens and are not stored.
  • Storage and memory totals reported by Android. Bitpurge keeps a daily record of your free space on the phone to draw the history chart and the home screen widget.

None of this leaves your phone. Bitpurge has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

Deleting and compressing files

When you clean photos, videos or audio, Bitpurge asks Android to move them to the system Trash. Android shows its own confirmation first and keeps the files for 30 days so you can restore them, unless you choose to delete them sooner.

When you compress a photo or video, Bitpurge saves a smaller copy on your phone and moves the original to the Trash the same way.

Files in folders you added, or picked from Downloads, are deleted permanently because Android has no Trash for them. Bitpurge asks you to confirm first.

Notifications

If you turn them on, Bitpurge shows a low-storage alert, a weekly cleanup reminder and a junk-file alert when your added folders hold 200 MB or more of junk. These are created on your phone from your storage totals; nothing is sent anywhere. While a long job runs (such as checking photos or compressing videos), a progress notification shows so the job can keep going when you switch apps.

Purchases

The optional Bitpurge Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. Bitpurge only learns whether your Google account owns Pro; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Bitpurge is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
