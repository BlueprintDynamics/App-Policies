Bitpurge Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

Bitpurge does not collect, store or share any of your personal data. Everything the app does happens on your phone.

What Bitpurge reads on your phone

  • Photos, videos and audio (only with your permission), to find large files, duplicates and old screenshots. Duplicate detection compares file contents on the phone itself.
  • App sizes and when you last used each app, only if you turn on "Usage access" for Bitpurge in Android Settings. This is used to show which apps take the most space.
  • Storage and memory totals reported by Android.

None of this leaves your phone. Bitpurge has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers of its own.

Deleting files

When you clean files, Bitpurge asks Android to move them to the system Trash. Android shows its own confirmation first and keeps the files for 30 days so you can restore them, unless you choose to delete them sooner.

Purchases

The optional Bitpurge Pro upgrade is sold through Google Play. Google processes the payment under Google's Privacy Policy. Bitpurge only learns whether your Google account owns Pro; it never sees your payment details.

Children

Bitpurge is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
