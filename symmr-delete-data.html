Delete your Symmr data

Symmr by Blueprint Dynamics LLC · Last updated: November 28, 2026

Symmr has no accounts. Almost everything lives on your phone, and you can delete it yourself in seconds. For the little that is kept on our side, email us and we delete it within 30 days.

Step 1: Delete the data on your phone

Your pantry, recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, favourites and any Kroger connection are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  1. On Android, open Settings › Apps › Symmr › Storage and tap Clear storage, or
  2. uninstall Symmr.

To remove Symmr's access to your Kroger account too, tap Disconnect in Symmr's ordering screen, or remove Symmr in your Kroger account settings.

Step 2: Ask us to delete what is kept on our side

  1. Before you clear the app, open Symmr, tap the Symmr Plus tile on the Home screen and note the App ID at the bottom of that screen.
  2. Email contact@blueprintdynamics.net with the subject "Delete my Symmr data" and include that App ID.
  3. We delete the data listed below within 30 days and reply to confirm. If you don't have the App ID, email us anyway and we will help.

What is deleted

  • Your subscription records held by our billing provider, RevenueCat, linked to your App ID (product, purchase dates, renewal status, country).
  • Your AI usage counts linked to your App ID. These are also deleted automatically within about 40 days.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Google Play purchase records are kept by Google under Google's own policies; we cannot delete them. Cancel subscriptions in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.
  • Daily anti-abuse counts per network (IP) address contain no app ID and are deleted automatically within 2 days.
  • Short-term technical logs kept by our hosting provider (such as IP addresses and request times) are deleted on their normal schedule, typically within 30 days.
  • Recipes, photos and text you send to AI features are never stored, so there is nothing to delete.

Deleting your data does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it first in Google Play if you no longer want to be billed.
