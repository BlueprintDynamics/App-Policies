Symmr Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026 · Terms of Use

Your kitchen data stays on your device. Symmr has no accounts, no ads and no tracking. Only the content you choose to send to an AI feature leaves your device, and Symmr does not keep it. To count AI use and check subscriptions, the server keeps a random app ID and a monthly count, nothing more.

Data stored on your device

Your pantry items, custom recipes, meal plans, grocery lists, kitchen equipment and favourites are saved on your device only. Symmr does not ask for a name, email address or password.

AI features

When you use the AI Chef, recipe customising, ingredient swaps, receipt or pantry scanning, or recipe import (from a link, pasted text or a photo), what you submit is sent to the Symmr server and processed by Google's Gemini AI service to produce the result. This can include:

  • the ingredients, preferences or recipe text you enter;
  • photos you choose of a recipe, a receipt or your pantry;
  • a recipe web link, which the Symmr server opens to read the recipe.

Symmr does not store this content after returning the result. Google processes it under the Gemini API Terms for paid services, under which Google does not use it to improve its products, and Google's Privacy Policy. To avoid paying twice for the same answer, the server may keep a text answer in short-term memory for up to 24 hours, linked only to the exact request, never to you. Like most web services, the server's hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs (such as IP addresses and request times) for security and reliability.

Browsing recipes, the meal planner, the pantry and grocery lists work without sending your data anywhere.

AI usage counts

Each copy of the app has a random ID created on your device (it is not your name, email or Google account). When you use an AI feature, the Symmr server records that ID and the number of AI actions used this month, so it can apply your plan's monthly allowance. To stop abuse of the free allowance, it also counts free AI actions per network (IP) address per day. These counts contain nothing you submitted and are deleted automatically within about 40 days (daily counts within 2 days).

Subscriptions

Symmr Plus and Pro are sold through Google Play. Google handles the payment; Symmr never sees your card or bank details. Purchases are managed with RevenueCat, which receives the app's random ID and the purchase records from Google Play (such as the product, purchase date, renewal status and country) so the app and server can tell whether your subscription is active.

Notifications

If you allow notifications, Symmr uses them only to tell you when a cooking timer finishes, including when the app is in the background, and for an optional morning reminder about pantry items expiring today or tomorrow (you can turn it off or change its time in the fridge list). They are created on your phone; nothing is sent to us. Android may also ask you to allow "Alarms & reminders" so timer alerts arrive on time; you can say no and still get them, possibly a little late.

Camera and photos

Symmr uses your camera or photo library only when you choose to scan or import from a photo, and only for the pictures you pick. It does not access your microphone, contacts or location. Read-aloud cooking steps use your device's built-in text-to-speech voice.

Backups

If you export a backup of your kitchen data, the file is saved or shared wherever you choose. Symmr does not upload it.

Ordering groceries

Ordering is optional and happens only when you tap it in your grocery list.

  • Instacart: the names and amounts of the items you choose are sent through the Symmr server to Instacart to create a shopping list page, which then opens on Instacart. Choosing a store, paying and delivery all happen with Instacart under its own terms and privacy policy.
  • Kroger and its stores (such as Harris Teeter, Ralphs and Fred Meyer): you sign in on Kroger's own page; Symmr never sees your password. Kroger then gives Symmr permission to search products and add items to your cart, and that permission is kept on your device. When you order, the item names, the store you picked and the products and quantities you chose are sent through the Symmr server to Kroger. Symmr cannot place orders, see your purchases or see your payment details, and the server does not keep any of this after the request. Checkout happens with the store under Kroger's privacy policy. Tap "Disconnect" in the ordering screen to remove the permission from your device, and you can also remove Symmr's access in your Kroger account.

Sharing and selling

Symmr does not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Grocery items are shared with Instacart or Kroger only when you ask Symmr to order them, as described above.

Deleting your data

Deleting your recipes and lists in the app, clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling Symmr removes your data from your device, including any Kroger connection. AI usage counts are deleted automatically as described above. To ask for your subscription records to be deleted, email us with the app ID shown on the Symmr Plus screen; Google Play keeps its own purchase records under Google's policies.

Health information

Nutrition figures and dietary suggestions are general information, not medical advice. Symmr does not collect health records.

Children

Symmr is not directed at children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
