Treehouse – Safe Browser for Kids: Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026 · Terms of Use

Treehouse does not collect any personal information from children or parents. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking, and nothing your child does in Treehouse is sent to us.

Who this app is for

Treehouse is a web browser for children, set up and controlled by a parent or guardian. Because it is used by children, it is designed to follow the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and Google Play's Families policies.

Information we do not collect

  • No names, email addresses, phone numbers or home addresses.
  • No advertising identifiers. The app has no advertising.
  • No location, contacts, camera or microphone access. Websites' requests for these are automatically refused.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services.

What is stored on the device

Parent settings (approved and blocked sites, screen-time limits, the parent PIN), bookmarks and the activity history shown to parents are saved only on the device running Treehouse. They are not sent to Blueprint Dynamics or anyone else.

Websites your child visits

Treehouse checks every address against its safety filters on the device before a page opens, and loads approved pages directly from those websites. Like any browser, this means the websites themselves receive normal technical information such as the device's IP address. Those sites are run by other companies and have their own privacy policies. Treehouse filters what pages are allowed to load, but cannot control what each website does.

Parental controls

Settings, approved-site editing and links that leave the app are protected by a parent PIN or an adult check. Parents can change or remove all settings at any time.

Deleting data

Parents can erase all Treehouse data at any time with the in-app data erasure option, by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. Since we hold no data, there is nothing for us to delete, but you are welcome to contact us with any questions.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date. We will never begin collecting children's personal information without first updating this policy and obtaining verifiable parental consent.
